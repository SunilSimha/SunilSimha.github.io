## Awards and Scholarships

### 2024

**JSPS Pre-doctoral Fellowship**
- Awarded ¥200,000, in addition to other covered expenses
- Supporting four-month research in Japan with Prof. K-G Lee on the analysis of cosmic baryons along FRB sightlines

### 2023

**UC Chancellor Dissertation Year Fellowship**
- Awarded $9,750 for research support during dissertation year

### 2021

**Newcomb-Cleveland Prize** - American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS)
- Team award of $25,000 for an outstanding research publication in *Science* journal
- For the research article: [A single fast radio burst localized to a massive galaxy at cosmological distance](https://dx.doi.org/10.1126/science.aaw5903)

### 2018

**UC Regents Graduate Fellowship**
- 1-year research fellowship for graduate students in the University of California system
- Award upon entry to UCSC graduate program

### 2017

**S N Bose Scholarship** - Indo-US Science and Technology Forum (IUSSTF)
- $2,000 grant for Indian students to conduct summer research projects in the USA

### 2013-2018

**Kishore Vigyan Protsahan Yojana (KVPY)** - Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India
- INR 80,000 per year
- For undergraduates who cleared the KVPY competitive examination and majored in pure science fields
- Awarded for all five years of BS-MS program at IIT Madras
