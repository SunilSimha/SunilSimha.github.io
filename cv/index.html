Research Experience

  • 2021 – Present
    The FLIMFLAM Survey
    Advisors: J. Xavier Prochaska (UCSC), Khee-Gan Lee (IPMU)
    FLIMFLAM is a redshift survey of foreground galaxies along Fast Radio Burst (FRB) sightlines to constrain key parameters describing the distribution of ionized matter in the CGM and IGM to ~20% precision.
    • Leading spectroscopic observational efforts with the Keck and Gemini telescopes for redshift estimation
    • Involved in imaging and spectroscopic observation, data reduction, redshift and mass estimation
    • Matter density map reconstruction and Bayesian inference of parameters describing matter distribution in the universe
  • 2018 – Present
    Optical Follow-up of Localized FRBs
    Advisor: J. Xavier Prochaska
    Current member of the F⁴ team. Involved in planning, execution and data reduction of observations of localized FRBs.
    • Work with CRAFT, MeerTRAP, REALFAST, and DSA collaborations
    • Use optical telescopes: Keck, Gemini, SOAR
    • FRB host galaxy identification and characterization
    • Study foreground matter along FRB sightlines
  • May – July 2022
    Keck Visiting Scholar
    Advisors: Percy Gomez (Keck), Sherry Yeh (Keck)
    Characterized the gratings and detector subsystems of the Low-Resolution Imaging Spectrograph (LRIS) on the Keck telescope.
    • Produced spectroscopic throughput measurements for three gratings
    • Automated the monthly checkout procedure for LRIS
  • 2020 – 2021
    LRIS Red-side Detector Upgrade
    Advisor: Constance Rockosi (UCSC)
    Involved in testing and commissioning of the optical CCD upgrade to LRIS on the Keck telescope.
    • Characterized CCD response through measurements of readout noise, gain and linearity
  • 2017 – 2018
    Mg II Absorber Clustering
    Advisors: Raghunathan Srianand (IUCAA), L. Sriram Kumar (IITM)
    Studied the clustering and distribution of metal absorption lines, specifically the Mg II doublet in quasar spectra.
    • Investigated masses of foreground halos contributing to absorption features
